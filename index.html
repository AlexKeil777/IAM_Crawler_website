A representative view
of the IAM market

The goal is a representative sample of aftermarket offers for BMW parts in a chosen country. The crawler discovers relevant shops and uses AI to extract comparable offers, prices and sources.

Compared with a typical fixed-shop crawler
Aspect Traditional crawler AIAM crawler
Page reading Extraction rules for each shop’s layout. AI reads different layouts and product descriptions without a template for each shop.
Offer validation Matches depend on predefined rules. Checks fit, quantity, variant and condition. Uncertain or unsuitable offers are excluded with a reason.
Market coverage A fixed shop list and crawl depth. Finds relevant shops, searches like a customer and stops at coverage targets.
Maintenance Update shop lists and repair changed parsers. Refreshes shop discovery and adapts to new layouts without shop-specific parsers.

How it works

  1. Discover relevant shops
  2. Search BMW part numbers
  3. Read and verify offers
  4. Export prices and sources

Shop access and geography can limit the sample. Representativeness is the goal, rather than exhaustive coverage.
